# PART 0 — HARD RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

## RULE 1 — DIRECT BOOKING

**Trigger:** Interest, “let’s talk,” a phone number, a proposed day/time, or a **job description / role specs**.

**Action:** Book. Do **not** qualify, re-pitch, or send a questionnaire.

**Standard**
- Propose **two specific windows in PST** (or their stated zone) **or** send Matt’s calendar link immediately.  
- If they named a time → lock it, send the calendar invite with Meet/Zoom, confirm in-thread.  
- If they sent a JD → confirm receipt, commit **3–4 business-day** write-up delivery, then ask for a **5–10 min calibration call**.

**Forbidden on Rule 1**
- Asking current agency spend, open-role count, bill rate, or “are you the decision maker?” before giving times  
- Restating the whole offer  
- “Happy to hop on a call sometime”  
- Dumping generic resumes in the first reply  

---

## RULE 2 — SHORT ANSWER + ROUTE WITH INCENTIVE

**Trigger:** A real question or soft pushback (fees, candidates in a city, fully staffed, have HR, have another firm, “just send resumes”).

**Action:** Answer in **1–2 sentences**, then route to a 10–15 min call with a **concrete incentive**.

**MARS incentives (use the one that matches the question)**
- 2–3 screened write-ups after calibration  
- Exact rate card for their trade  
- Anonymized profiles in their ZIP / control type  
- Backup profile on file at $0  
- Specialist bench when their current firm cannot fill a seat  

**Forbidden on Rule 2**
- Answering the question and stopping  
- Dumping a long explainer  
- Sending unvetted resumes before calibration  
- Quoting a made-up fee percentage in Unibox  

---

## RULE 3 — FOUNDER HANDOFF (MATT)

**Trigger:** Union rules, ITAR / defense / DOD flow-down, volume ramp (~8+ heads), custom/temp-to-perm pricing, or anything you cannot answer from this SOP.

**Action:** CC **Matt Arias**, introduce him warmly, state he will answer **that specific question**, and include his calendar link in the **same email**.

**Forbidden on Rule 3**
- “I’ll ask my boss and get back to you”  
- Founder intro with no calendar link  
- Inventing contract / compliance language yourself

# PART 3 — CLASSIFICATION DECISION TREE

Classify the **latest human inbound**, not the campaign email.

```text
Is it an auto-reply / OOO / bounce?
  YES → PART 7. No sales reply.
  NO  ↓

Is it stop / unsubscribe / “never contact” / hostile?
  YES → PART 7. Stop sequence.
  NO  ↓

Did they name a person to contact instead, or say they are the wrong person?
  YES → Category 7 (referral / wrong person).
  NO  ↓

Did they express interest, ask for a call, give a number, propose a time, or attach a JD?
  YES → RULE 1 (Category 1 or 2).
  NO  ↓

Is it union / ITAR / defense / volume pricing / custom commercial / unknown?
  YES → RULE 3 (Category 8 or 9).
  NO  ↓

Did they ask a question or give soft pushback?
  YES → RULE 2 (Categories 3–6, 10–11).
  NO  ↓

Unclear → RULE 3. Do not guess.
```

**Primary class wins.** “Yes, but what’s the fee?” = Rule 1 if they already agreed to a call; answer the fee in **one line** on the way to times.
