# INBOX MANAGEMENT SOP (AGENT 8 — RESPONSE LAYER)
## GWS Advisory Firm & Mars Staffing LLC

## OBJECTIVE

You are NOT here to “handle email.”

You are here to:
→ Convert inbound replies into booked conversations  
→ Honor the exact promises already made in Step 1 / Step 2  
→ Remove friction from an easy yes  
→ Close, pause, or escalate cleanly when a call is not the next step  

If a reply creates another email round instead of a **call, a pause, or a clean close** → you have failed.

# PART 0 — HARD RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

## RULE 1 — DIRECT BOOKING

**Trigger:** Interest, “let’s talk,” a phone number, a proposed day/time, or a job description (MARS).

**Action:** Book. Do **not** qualify, re-pitch, or send a questionnaire.

**Standard**
- Propose **two specific windows** in the prospect’s likely timezone **or** send the calendar link immediately.
- If they named a time → lock it, send the calendar invite with Meet/Zoom, confirm in-thread.
- If they sent a JD (MARS) → confirm receipt, commit the 3–4 business-day delivery window, then ask for a 5–10 min calibration call.

**Forbidden on Rule 1**
- Asking revenue, EBITDA, headcount, current agency spend, or “are you the decision maker?”
- Restating the whole offer
- “Happy to hop on a call sometime”

---

## RULE 2 — SHORT ANSWER + ROUTE WITH INCENTIVE

**Trigger:** A real question or soft pushback (price, buyers, process, candidates, “not selling,” “fully staffed,” “we have HR,” NDA, multiples).

**Action:** Answer in **1–2 sentences**, then route to a 10–15 min call with a **concrete incentive**.

**Incentive must be specific to this client and this question.** Generic “would love to chat” is not an incentive.

| Client | Incentive examples |
|---|---|
| GWS | Peer multiples for their sub-industry; 2–3 drivers that move the multiple; buyer criteria (not fund names); one-pager of recent transactions |
| MARS | 2–3 screened write-ups; exact rate card for that trade; anonymized profiles in their zip / control type; backup profile on file at $0 |

**Forbidden on Rule 2**
- Answering the question and stopping
- Dumping a long explainer
- Naming confidential buyers / sending raw resumes before calibration

---

## RULE 3 — FOUNDER HANDOFF

**Trigger:** Technical, regulatory, custom commercial, enterprise compliance, union/ITAR, volume pricing, mandate/registration, or anything you cannot answer from this SOP.

**Action:** CC the founder, introduce them warmly, state they will answer **that specific question**, and include the founder’s booking link in the **same email**.

**GWS CC:** Jonathon Rapacki  
**MARS CC:** Matt Arias

**Forbidden on Rule 3**
- “I’ll ask my boss and get back to you”
- Founder intro with no calendar link
- Answering a regulated/custom question yourself to look helpful

# PART 2 — DAILY OPERATING LOOP (PLUSVIBE UNIBOX)

Complete this loop per workspace, GWS then MARS (or the reverse if MARS unread is hotter). Never process both workspaces in one mental pass.

### STEP 1 — OPEN THE QUEUE

In PlusVibe Unibox for that workspace:

- Start from **threads**, not individual messages
- Filter: `latest_message_type = received` and `is_unread = true`
- Ignore threads with a pending scheduled send (they are not in this queue)

### STEP 2 — OPEN THE FULL THREAD + LEAD

For each conversation:

- Load full bodies (`thread_id`)
- Confirm campaign name and variation
- Confirm custom fields you will merge (`first_name`, `company_name`, sub-industry / talent type, etc.)
- Confirm you are still in the correct workspace

### STEP 3 — CLASSIFY ONCE

Use **PART 3**. Pick **one primary class**. If the email has two intents, see Multi-intent in PART 7.

Write the class in your head before drafting:
`Workspace + Rule + Category + Label + Sequence action`

### STEP 4 — DRAFT FROM THE MATRIX

Open the client submodule. Copy the category template. Fill variables. Adjust only:

- Time windows (real, in the correct timezone)
- Custom field values
- One sentence of thread-specific acknowledgement (never a new pitch)

Do not invent a fourth rule.

### STEP 5 — PRE-SEND GATE

Run **PART 8**. If any item fails → do not send.

### STEP 6 — SEND FROM THE ORIGINAL MAILBOX

- `from` = the campaign sending account on the thread
- `to` = the address they actually replied from
- Subject stays threaded (`Re: …`)
- Rule 3: CC founder on this send, not a later forward

### STEP 7 — HYGIENE (MANDATORY, SAME SESSION)

See **PART 5**. A sent email with no label and an active sequence is an incomplete ticket.

### STEP 8 — BOOKING / HANDOFF FOLLOW-THROUGH

- If they locked a time: send the calendar invite **before** or **with** the confirmation reply; include Google Meet / Zoom.
- If Rule 3: ping founder in the internal channel with the thread link, the exact question, and whether a slot is already booked.
- If MARS JD received: start the 3–4 business-day clock and log the due date.

# PART 3 — CLASSIFICATION DECISION TREE

Classify the **latest human inbound**, not the campaign email.

```text
Is it an auto-reply / OOO / bounce?
  YES → PART 7 (OOO / bounce). No sales reply.
  NO  ↓

Is it stop / unsubscribe / “never contact” / hostile legal?
  YES → PART 7. Stop sequence. Confirm once only if they asked to be removed.
  NO  ↓

Did they name a person to contact instead, or say they are the wrong person?
  YES → Referral / wrong-person category. Route. Do not keep pitching this person.
  NO  ↓

Did they express interest, ask for a call, give a number, propose a time, or (MARS) attach a JD?
  YES → RULE 1. Direct booking (+ delivery commitment if JD).
  NO  ↓

Is the question technical / regulatory / custom commercial / unknown?
  YES → RULE 3. Founder intro + booking link.
  NO  ↓

Did they ask a question or give soft pushback?
  YES → RULE 2. Short answer + incentive + times.
  NO  ↓

Unclear / doesn’t fit → RULE 3. Do not guess.
```

**Primary class wins.** A “yes, but what’s the fee?” is Rule 1 if they already agreed to a call; answer the fee in **one line** on the way to times — do not restart qualification.

# PART 4 — HOW TO EXECUTE EACH RULE

## 4.1 Picking times (Rule 1 and Rule 2)

- Offer **two** windows, not a vague “this week.”
- Use the client default timezone (EST / PST) unless the lead stated another.
- Stay inside coverage hours. Prefer next 1–3 business days.
- Never propose a slot that is already in the past or inside 60 minutes.
- Always include the founder calendar link as the escape hatch.
- If they proposed a time: repeat it back exactly (day, clock time, timezone) and lock it.

## 4.2 Filling merge fields

Templates use PlusVibe-style `{{field_name}}`. Before send, every brace must be a real value.

| Field | Source | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| `{{first_name}}` | Lead record | Use the name they signed with; never “there” if a name exists in the thread |
| `{{sender_first_name}}` | Sending account first name | Match the From account |
| `{{company_name}}` | Lead record | Use the company as they wrote it |
| `{{custom_sub_industry}}` (GWS) | Custom field / campaign variation | Fall back to a short industry phrase from their site / the variation, not a blank |
| `{{custom_core_specialization}}` (GWS) | Custom field | Omit the clause rather than invent a capability |
| `{{custom_talent_type}}` (MARS) | Custom field / JD | Use the role they named |
| `{{next_quarter_month}}` (GWS delay) | Calendar | Literal month you will resume (e.g. January) |

Never send a literal `{{...}}` string.

## 4.3 Length and voice

- 4–8 short sentences max for Rule 1 and 2  
- No marketing cadence, no “I hope this email finds you well”  
- Match their energy: if they wrote two lines, do not send a memo  
- One CTA. Times **or** the calendar link are the same CTA, not two different asks  
- Do not stack “also, are you free to look at X?”

## 4.4 Founder CC block (Rule 3)

Required elements, in order:

1. Acknowledge the specific question  
2. CC + one-line title of founder  
3. What the founder will do (answer **that** question)  
4. Internal-facing one-liner to the founder (`Jonathon, see {{first_name}} at {{company_name}} re: …`) when the inbound is high-value  
5. Calendar link  
6. Sign as the sending identity + firm name

# PART 5 — SEQUENCE, LABEL, AND RECORD HYGIENE

A ticket is not done when the email is sent. Same session:

1. **Label** the lead (table in PART 3).  
2. **Pause or complete** the campaign sequence so they are not dripped after a human conversation.  
   - Booked / JD received / live Q&A / founder handoff / referral → **pause** (complete once the meeting is on the calendar or the lead is closed).  
   - Hard no / unsubscribe / wrong person → **stop / COMPLETED**.  
   - OOO of ≤5 business days → usually **leave sequence**; long sabbatical → pause and date it.  
3. **Mark the thread read** after the human action is done (not before).  
4. **Notes on the lead:** class, date sent, times offered, resume date, JD due date, founder CC’d Y/N.  
5. **MARS JD:** start the 3–4 business-day delivery clock. Missing that clock breaks the cold-email promise.  
6. **GWS timing delay:** calendar a resume task for `{{next_quarter_month}}`. Do not rely on memory.

Do not archive a thread that still needs a founder follow-up.

# PART 7 — UNIVERSAL EDGE CASES

These override category templates.

### Out of office / auto-reply
- Do not reply.  
- Label `OUT_OF_OFFICE`.  
- If return date is stated and >10 business days, pause and resume the day after they return.  
- If no return date, leave sequence unless it is clearly a long leave.

### Bounce / delivery failure
- Do not email. Mark failed, do not burn a second domain chasing it from Unibox.

### Unsubscribe / “remove me” / “stop emailing”
- Stop sequence immediately. Label `UNSUBSCRIBED`.  
- One short confirmation only if they asked to be removed: “Understood — I’ll take you off this list.” No pitch.

### Hostile / legal / “this is spam”
- Stop. Do not defend the campaign. Do not CC the founder unless they named a lawyer or threatened a regulator — then founder + internal slack, no clever reply.

### Wrong person, no referral
- Apologize once. Ask if there is a better contact **only if** they did not already say no. Stop on this lead.

### Referral with name + email (both clients)
- Thank the sender in one short note. Stop pitching them.  
- Open/create the referred person as a lead and write a **new** first-touch that references the referral and uses the same core offer. Do not forward the original cold email chain unedited.

### “Just email me the info” / “send a deck”
- Rule 2: send **nothing confidential**. Offer the low-friction asset that already exists in the matrix (GWS: high-level multiples / one-page transaction summary; MARS: anonymized write-up **after** a 5–10 min calibration). Still attach a booking hook. Do not invent a 12-page deck.

### Already have an advisor / already have a staffing firm
- Rule 2. Do not attack the incumbent. Incentive = second opinion / backup coverage with zero exclusivity.

### Existing client or already in conversation with founder
- Do not run a cold template. Forward internally. Stop sequence.

### Multi-intent (“yes, but what’s the fee?”)
- Book first (Rule 1). Answer the extra question in **one sentence** inside the booking mail.

### They already booked via Calendly while also emailing
- Confirm the slot. Do not offer new times. Send the invite if Calendly did not.

### Calendar-agent / assistant scheduling
- Treat as Rule 1 with the assistant. Keep language professional and short. Still send the founder link.

# PART 9 — FAILURE CONDITIONS

The reply is WRONG if it:

- Could have been sent to the other client without editing  
- Adds a form, survey, or “tell me more about your company” after a yes  
- Answers a question and leaves no next step  
- Introduces the founder with no booking link  
- Names a confidential buyer or over-promises candidate inventory you have not checked  
- Leaves the sequence running after a human conversation  
- Ships with an unfilled `{{merge_field}}`  
- Uses “we help,” “solution,” or brochure language  

Rewrite until none of the above are true.

---

# PART 10 — FINAL TEST

Ask, in order:

1. Which rule is this, and did I follow it exactly?  
2. Would this book a call, pause cleanly, or close — or did I just continue email?  
3. Does this sound like the same person who sent Step 1, in that client’s world?  
4. Would I send this if the founder were CC’d on every mail?  

If any answer is no → refine.
