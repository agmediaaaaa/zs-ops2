## What Codicen does (plain)

Builds a **deadline calendar** for that contractor’s situation and **texts + emails** the office **60 / 30 / 7 days** before each date.

They **track and remind**. They do **not** file renewals, pay bonds, sell coverage, give legal/tax advice, or work for the state.

Site positioning: *They don’t take your license for bad work. They take it for paperwork.*  
Coverage line: **Nine fronts. One calendar. Yours.**

### Nine fronts ([codicen.com](https://codicen.com))

1. **CSLB** — license, contractor bond, coverage certificate/exemption, LLC worker bond + liability, salesperson reg, 90-day address/personnel/qualifier notices  
2. **Secretary of State** — Statement of Information (LLC-12 / SI-550)  
3. **Franchise Tax Board** — $800 annual tax, LLC gross-receipts fee, entity returns/estimates  
4. **IRS & EDD** — 941 / DE 9, W-2 / 940, 1099-NEC, DE 34, DE 542, federal entity return  
5. **Cities & County** — business license in home city **and every city you work**, DBA, per-permit city taxes  
6. **EPA** — firm RRP (5-year), individual renovator + refresher, 90-day window  
7. **DPR + Ag** — applicator, CE, pest business license, county Ag reg, monthly use reports  
8. **DIR / Cal-OSHA / CARB** — public works registration, certified payroll, Form 300A, Clean Truck Check  
9. **Carriers** — WC renewal + audit, GL, commercial auto, surety term  

Product also surfaces **fee + miss cost + statute** per date (site description).

### How it works (shareable)

1. Intro call (30 min): demo + understand situation  
2. Pricing review (on call only)  
3. After agreement: build 12-month deadline calendar from license #, trade certs, city licenses, etc.  
4. Second call: walk every deadline, who wants it, what a miss costs  
5. Text + email 60 / 30 / 7 days before each deadline

## Proof / citations (general industry — not “their file”)

- July 1, 2023: CSLB suspended 1,681 licenses and pulled a class from 1,122 more (no WC on file)

- Wright v. Issak (2007) — mid-job coverage gap, lost right to be paid

- Labor Code 3722 — uninsured employer stop-order penalty framed at $1,500 per employee  
- Site story: bond cancel/run-out → same-day suspend → work while out can mean full fee disgorgement (B&P 7031)
