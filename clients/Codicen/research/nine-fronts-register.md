# Research — Nine fronts (what Codicen can report on)

Source: [codicen.com](https://codicen.com) Coverage section + onboarding.

**Rule for outbound:** Email 1 sells **hard** fronts. Public CSLB license/bond/coverage dates are the **easy proof after yes**, not the offer.

## Easy (public CSLB file) — Email 2 proof only

| Item | Notes |
|------|--------|
| License expiration | Master license file |
| Bond expiration | Surety on record optional |
| Coverage on file | Say “coverage on file,” not “comp,” in cold email |
| Class / qualifier | Ops / personalization, not Email 1 dump |

## Hard (Codicen register) — Email 1 + call build

| Front | Reportable examples |
|-------|---------------------|
| CSLB (beyond the three) | LLC worker bond + liability, salesperson reg, 90-day address/personnel/qualifier notices |
| Secretary of State | SOI LLC-12 (2yr) / SI-550 (yearly) |
| Franchise Tax Board | $800 annual tax, LLC gross-receipts fee tiers, entity returns/estimates |
| IRS & EDD | 941 / DE 9, W-2 / 940, 1099-NEC, DE 34, DE 542 |
| Cities & County | Business license in every city worked, DBA cycle, per-permit taxes |
| EPA | Firm RRP (5yr), renovator + refresher, 90-day window |
| DPR + Ag | Applicator, CE, pest business license, county Ag, monthly use reports |
| DIR / Cal-OSHA / CARB | Public works registration, certified payroll, Form 300A, Clean Truck Check |
| Carriers | Policy renewals + premium audit, GL, commercial auto, surety term |

Also per date when built: **fee**, **miss cost**, **statute source**. Reminders: **60 / 30 / 7**.

Hard fronts need a short call (cities worked, public-works or pre-1978 work, entity, crew shape). Do not pretend the full nine-front map emails cold with zero input.
